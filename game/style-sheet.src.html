Order Up

A short-order cook's week at The Café in Leavenworth, Kansas. One shift a day, Monday to Friday, built for a phone held in one hand. The words that go with this sheet are in game/README.md.

The run

One week is one run. Each day is a 6am – 2pm shift, about 90 seconds. Breakfast first, lunch and the day's special from 11am, then after close: spend the tips, pick a card. Three spilled coffees in a day and the week is over.

Mon

Breakfast tickets, one easy special. Learn the flat-top.

Tue

Lunch gets busier. To-go tickets in clamshells.

Wed

Wraps join the pool. Hot sandwiches after a finished week.

Thu

Two specials can be on at once. The rail fills.

Fri

Ends on a catering order: one big ticket of platters, its own clock.

Colour

The house palette keeps its jobs. Red means only two things in the game: tap me (a button) and late (a ticket's timer), the kitchen board's own rule. The game palette is for food, steel and sky, never for a house job.

Sprites

Flat, chunky, rounded. One colour per part and one flat highlight. Every object has the house outline (teal dark, never black) and the house shadow: one flat offset down and to the right, no blur. Drawn in code, so the game loads no image files.

Do and don't

Do

  • Draw this kitchen: steel tables, the flat-top, white plates, the pickle spear, clamshells
  • Teal-dark outline and one flat shadow on everything
  • Show raw, done and burnt by shape as well as colour
  • Use the menu's own names for food
  • Respect reduced motion; keep sound off until it's turned on

Don't

  • Draw people: the staff don't want their likeness online
  • Put red on teal, or red on anything that isn't a button or a late ticket
  • Use emoji, prizes for scores, ads, accounts or a leaderboard server
  • Show fries or deep-fried food, evenings or weekends
  • Show menu prices: tips are game money
